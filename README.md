# 📊 Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases**. The objective is to understand customer spending patterns, purchasing frequency, customer segments, product/category performance, and other behavioral trends.

The analysis combines **Python, SQL/SQLite, and Power BI** to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

The ultimate goal is to help businesses better understand their customers and make **data-driven decisions for sales, marketing, customer retention, and future growth strategies**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

* Understand overall customer purchasing behavior.
* Identify high-value and high-spending customer segments.
* Analyze customer behavior across different age groups.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Analyze purchasing frequency and customer preferences.
* Understand the relationship between discounts and purchasing behavior.
* Identify opportunities for customer retention and targeted marketing.
* Provide actionable recommendations for future sales growth.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

1. Which customer segments contribute the most to revenue?
2. Which age groups have the highest spending?
3. Which product categories perform the best?
4. What is the average purchase amount?
5. How frequently do customers make purchases?
6. Which customers can be considered high-value customers?
7. How do discounts influence customer purchasing behavior?
8. Which categories or customer segments should receive more attention?
9. What strategies can help improve customer engagement and retention?

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project follows a structured data analytics workflow:

### 1️⃣ Data Loading

The dataset was loaded into **Jupyter Notebook using Python and Pandas**.

The initial analysis included:

* Understanding the dataset structure.
* Checking columns and data types.
* Identifying missing values.
* Checking duplicate records.
* Understanding categorical and numerical variables.

### 2️⃣ Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis.

Activities included:

* Handling missing values.
* Checking and removing duplicate records where required.
* Correcting inconsistent data.
* Formatting dates and columns.
* Standardizing column names.
* Creating calculated fields.
* Preparing the dataset for SQL analysis.

### 3️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis

Python was used to explore customer behavior and identify initial patterns.

The analysis included:

* Customer spending patterns.
* Age-group analysis.
* Purchase frequency.
* Product/category performance.
* Customer segmentation.
* Review and discount-related analysis.

### 4️⃣ SQL Analysis

The cleaned data was connected to **SQLite** for further business analysis.

SQL queries were used to calculate and summarize important metrics such as:

* Total revenue.
* Average purchase amount.
* Sales by category.
* Customer spending.
* Age-group performance.
* Purchase frequency.
* Customer segmentation.
* Top-performing categories/products.

SQL helped convert the cleaned transactional data into **business-focused summaries and KPIs**.

### 5️⃣ Power BI Dashboard

The analyzed data was then connected to **Power BI** to create an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard presents important KPIs and visualizations in a simple format so that both technical and non-technical stakeholders can understand the insights quickly.

### 📊 Dashboard Includes

* Total Customers
* Total Revenue
* Average Purchase Amount
* Average Review Rating
* Sales by Category
* Customer Segmentation
* Age Group Analysis
* Purchase Frequency
* Product/Category Performance
* Discount-related Analysis

Based on the analysis, businesses can consider the following strategies:

Identify high-value customers and create targeted offers and loyalty programs to improve retention.

Focus marketing and promotional efforts on high-performing categories while identifying opportunities to improve underperforming categories.

### 📢 Targeted Marketing

Use customer demographics, spending patterns, and purchase frequency to create more personalized marketing campaigns.

### 💰 Discount Optimization

Analyze discount usage and customer response to ensure promotions generate additional sales without unnecessarily reducing revenue.
